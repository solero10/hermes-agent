## Important decisions

- Exact-only/degraded dedupe stays in `Shaped` until semantic dedupe runs.
- `Ready for CortexDB` and `Deduped` require full semantic dedupe coverage unless already imported.
- `Deduped` is reserved for structured duplicate/merged/exact-duplicate evidence, not prose that merely mentions duplicate work.
- Useful durable personal/relationship/value/therapy context should not be stopped just because it is non-actionable; it should remain eligible after semantic dedupe.
- Policy cards should use compact tags:
  - `Reference / merge`: useful, but should merge into broader context rather than stand alone
  - `Obsolete / skip`: stale/time-bound/expired/completed/no longer durable
  - `Needs validation`: potentially useful but source evidence is weak
  - `Policy hold`: generic catch-all for vague/stale/secondhand/missing identifier/current-task safety concerns
- Abbreviated Kanban cards should stay clean. Stage and lineage remain detail-only.
